Privacy Policy

Fudoku · Last updated: 22 September 2026 Fudoku plans your week of meals, prices the dishes and turns them into a shopping list. This page says exactly what the app does with your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you answer during setup and everything the app builds from it:

  • your name, household size, days and meals per day
  • your diet, allergens, disliked ingredients, appliances and cooking time
  • your budget and the shop you picked
  • your height, weight, age, sex and activity, only if you asked the app

to work out a calorie target;

  • the plan itself, your shopping list and what you have cooked.

This is stored in the app's own container on your iPhone. It is not sent to us, and we have no server that could receive it. Delete the app and it is gone with it.

What leaves your device

  • Purchases. If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment. We use

RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. They receive an anonymous identifier generated by the app and the receipt Apple issues — never your name, your plan or your shopping list. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

  • Sending a list to a shop. Only if you tap it yourself. The ingredients

and quantities from that list are sent to the shop's service so it can fill a cart. Nothing else goes with them.

  • Notifications. Scheduled and shown by iOS on your device. Nothing is

sent anywhere.

What we never collect

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites, no accounts, no email address. The app works without signing in because there is nothing to sign in to.

Children

Fudoku is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you control it directly: change your answers on the You tab, or delete the app to erase everything. Purchases are managed in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version appears at this address.
