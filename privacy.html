Privacy Policy

Fudoku · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Fudoku plans your week of meals, estimates what the dishes cost and turns them into a shopping list. This page says exactly what the app does with your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you answer during setup and everything the app builds from it:

  • your name and ZIP code, if you choose to give them;
  • household size, days and meals per day;
  • your diet, allergens, disliked ingredients, appliances and cooking time;
  • your budget and the shop you picked;
  • your height, weight, age, sex and activity — only if you ask the app to work out a calorie target;
  • the plan itself, your shopping list and what you have cooked.

This is stored in the app's own container on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that could receive it. Delete the app and it is gone with it.

What leaves your device

Subscriptions. Apple processes every payment. The app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, to check whether your subscription is active. Each time the app starts, RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated by the app, your purchase history from Apple (receipts and transactions) and basic technical details such as the app version, device model, iOS version and IP address. It never receives your name, your answers, your plan or your shopping list. We use this data to give you access to what you paid for and to see totals such as how many people subscribed. RevenueCat processes it for us under its own privacy policy and data-processing terms, which protect it at least as well as this policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Notifications. The trial reminder and cooking timers are scheduled and shown by iOS on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

What we never collect

No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no accounts and no email address. The app works without signing in because there is nothing to sign in to.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you change it on the You tab or delete the app. RevenueCat keeps the anonymous identifier and purchase records for as long as they are needed to provide subscription status and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

You control the data on your device directly: change your answers on the You tab, or delete the app to erase everything. To ask for access to, or deletion of, the purchase data RevenueCat holds for your copy of the app, email us with the order ID from your App Store receipt and we will handle it with RevenueCat. Subscriptions are managed in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Children

Fudoku is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them. Calorie targets are meant for adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version appears at this address.
