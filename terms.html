The app

Fudoku builds meal plans, estimates what they cost and turns them into a shopping list. You may use it on the Apple devices you own, under the rules Apple sets for apps bought from the App Store.

Subscription

The plan, the recipes, dish swaps, the price estimates and the shopping list are free. A Fudoku Pro subscription adds cooking mode: step-by-step cooking with timers and progress through a dish.

  • Yearly — $39.90 a year. New subscribers may get the first 3 days free. The purchase screen shows whether the free trial applies to your Apple Account before you pay; if you have had it before, you are charged when you subscribe.
  • Weekly — $4.90 a week, no free period.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends; the renewal is charged within 24 hours before that moment. Cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end. If you have a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid being charged — that is how Apple's billing works, and it is why the app offers to remind you before then. Prices may differ by country and may change; a change never applies to a period you have already paid for.

What the numbers mean

Prices of ingredients are our estimates, not the shop's till. They come from our own price table, adjusted for the chain you picked, and are there to compare plans — not to promise a total. Nutrition figures come from USDA FoodData Central and are scaled to your portions. They are an estimate, not a label, and Fudoku is not medical advice. If you have an allergy or a medical condition, read the packaging and talk to a professional: the app filters recipes by their ingredient lists, it cannot know what is in the packet you bought.

Photographs

Dish photographs in the app are illustrative and may differ from the dish you cook.
